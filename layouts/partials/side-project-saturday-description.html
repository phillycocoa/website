Side Project Saturday is a chance to work on your iOS or Mac projects in a group setting. Get help with your app, share your ideas, get feedback, and socialize with other local Apple developers. Beginners are always welcome! Come and go as you please.

Join us in person at the Willow Grove GIANT Community Center. For virtual attendance, check in on Slack in #side-project-saturday after 10:00am for a status update and any Zoom link. A virtual connection is not guaranteed. Join our Slack community.
